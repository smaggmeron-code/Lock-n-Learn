What the app does · 這個 App 做什麼

A parent picks apps by name (YouTube, Roblox, and so on). Those apps stay shielded until the child writes a character. Completely Blocked apps stay closed until a parent turns that off.

家長用名稱點選要鎖的 App。孩子寫一個字才能打開。完全封鎖的 App 要家長關掉才會開。

Do not pick a category such as Games, Entertainment, or All Apps. Do not pick Phone or Settings.

請不要選擇「遊戲」、「娛樂」或「所有 App」等類別。不要選電話或設定。

Parent PIN · 家長密碼

Settings open with the 4-digit PIN you set at first launch. That PIN is stored on the device. It is not Apple’s Screen Time passcode and not the iPhone/iPad unlock code.

家長設定用一開始設定的 4 位數字。密碼只存在這台裝置上。不是螢幕使用時間密碼，也不是解鎖手機的密碼。

Limits · 限制

  • This app does not replace the iOS lock screen. · 不會取代 iOS 鎖定畫面。
  • Apple cannot re-lock in under 15 minutes. · Apple 最快 15 分鐘後才能再鎖上。
  • The lock is strongest on a child’s Apple ID in Family Sharing. · 孩子用家庭分享的 Apple ID 時，鎖定最穩。
