What the app does · 這個 App 做什麼

A parent chooses which apps to lock. Those apps stay shielded until the child writes. Off-limits apps stay closed until a parent turns that off.

家長選好要鎖的 App，孩子寫字才能打開。禁用 App 要家長關掉才會開。

You can pick single apps (YouTube, Roblox, and so on), whole categories, or both. Picking Games, Social and Entertainment locks every app in them, including apps installed later; when the child unlocks, a category opens all its apps together. Don't pick Phone or Settings.

可以一個一個點選 App（例如 YouTube、Roblox），也可以選整個類別，或兩種一起用。選「遊戲」、「社交」和「娛樂」，裡面所有的 App 都會鎖住，之後新裝的也一樣；解鎖時，同一類別的 App 會一起打開。不要選電話或設定。

Parent PIN · 家長密碼

Settings open with the 4-digit PIN you set at first launch. That PIN is stored on the device. It is not Apple’s Screen Time passcode and not the iPhone/iPad unlock code.

家長設定用一開始設定的 4 位數字。密碼只存在這台裝置上。不是螢幕使用時間密碼，也不是解鎖手機的密碼。

Limits · 限制

  • This app does not replace the iOS lock screen. · 不會取代 iOS 鎖定畫面。
  • Apple cannot re-lock in under 15 minutes. · Apple 最快 15 分鐘後才能再鎖上。
  • The lock is strongest on a child’s Apple ID in Family Sharing. · 孩子用家庭分享的 Apple ID 時，鎖定最穩。
